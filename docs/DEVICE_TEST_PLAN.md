# Required device gates

1. Install signed APK on Vivo V20 SE / Android 12. Pair once; close/reopen and reboot without losing identity/profile.
2. Navigate, observe, click/fill with fresh refs, reject stale refs, open/change/close tabs, choose an upload and verify download state.
3. Open WhatsApp or ChatGPT while Hey browses. Confirm its private display continues rendering, unrelated phone pixels are absent and both users remain independent.
4. Use a known one-minute audiovisual fixture. Observe from zero through ended state; fetch all frames and WAV chunks, measure visual/audio timestamp correspondence, include silent intervals and scene changes.
5. Revoke audio consent, block capture, interrupt networking, buffer and seek. Verify gaps remain visible and no false full-coverage claim appears.
6. Configure Firebase; stop the idle service, submit a task from ChatGPT and prove actual high-priority delivery, wake and task execution. Test normal priority, Doze, force-stop and Vivo background settings separately.
7. Disconnect after a consequential action; verify no automatic repeat. Restart service; inspect UNKNOWN outcome and exact action receipt.
8. Exercise manual takeover, stale status, process termination, cancellation and session recovery.
9. Connect the real ChatGPT plugin through OAuth, inspect tool discovery and verify that target host accepts returned JPEG and WAV content.
10. Record 60-minute mixed-use and long-video evidence, memory/battery/network measurements and release-signing continuity. The full product remains open until required gates pass.
